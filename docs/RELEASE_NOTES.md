# XPTablet 1.0

The first release of XPTablet, a free cockpit tablet for X-Plane 12 by Alpine Aviation.

**Install:** download `XPTablet-1.0.zip` below, unzip it, and copy the `XPTablet` folder into `X-Plane 12/Resources/plugins/`.
XPTablet also installs and updates through the SkunkCrafts Updater.

**Manual:** `XPTablet/docs/XPTablet_User_Manual.pdf`, also in the tablet's Documents app.

## In 1.0

- On the tablet of any aircraft built for AviTab, with no change to the aircraft; a floating window (also in VR) on the rest.
- Moving map with X-Plane and VATSIM traffic, airports down to the stands (Select Parking), your SimBrief route and procedures, VATSIM airspaces.
- Airports and frequencies (tap to tune COM1 standby), SimBrief Flight Plan and the Dispatch briefing by section, weather and METARs.
- openSAM jetways, BetterPushback or X-Plane's tug, ToLiss cabin lights and slides.
- Checklists, documents, ACARS (Hoppie), logbook, timers, notes, navaid search, xPilot controls.
- App Store with charts, flight planning, network and music web apps; a browser with tabs (Windows and Linux).
- Media controls, themes (with Theme-Colored Apps), games, and Local Connect for a phone or iPad.

## Since 0.9.1

- Traffic is colored only within 6 nm, and a resolution advisory only within 3 nm.
- Dispatch tabs with tap-to-find in the briefing PDF; Discord; Select Parking; one tap opens a map card; xPilot's four buttons.
- Fixed: a crash when a Local Connect device connected before the tablet was first drawn; the Settings sidebar flicker.
- Aircraft that look for AviTab by its plugin signature (Aerobask DA42) switch tablet pages; WXPlane's icon kept off the tablet.
- The ToLiss cockpit screen filled to its edges; openSAM jetways docked again after the beacon goes off.
